The PLAITS engine (FELUCCA_ENG_PLAITS) is ported from Emilie Gillet's eurorack code
<https://github.com/pichenettes/eurorack> (commit f4cfa76, MIT):

- firmware/src/engines/plaits/eng_plaits.c: Plaits' waveshaping, additive, granular formant and
  particle models (plaits/dsp/engine/waveshaping_engine.cc, additive_engine.cc, grain_engine.cc,
  particle_engine.cc; plaits/dsp/oscillator/harmonic_oscillator.h, grainlet_oscillator.h,
  z_oscillator.h; plaits/dsp/noise/particle.h)
- firmware/src/engines/plaits/plaits_tables.h and tools/gen_plaits_tables.py: the waveshaper and
  wavefolder tables (plaits/resources/lookup_tables.py)

Ported to fixed-point C for Optimist, 2026. The ported files stay under the MIT licence below; the
rest of Optimist is GPL-3.0-only (LICENSE). "Plaits" names the source; Optimist is not affiliated
with Mutable Instruments.
