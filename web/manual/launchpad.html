New: tried on an FM-1 with a Launchpad X (2026-11-26).

Setup

  • A Novation Launchpad X and the FM-1 on a powered USB hub: the FM-1 gives the port no power.
  • SYSTEM > USB, MODE HOST. Meanwhile no computer on that port (no editor, no USB audio).
  • Builder items USB_HOST and LAUNCHPAD.

What it does

The Launchpad app of jank, the same one that plays a Digitone II, with the FM-1 as its synth.

  • Tracks: T1 to T3, the drum track, then each drum lane as a track of its own.
  • Play: the pads play the selected track in its scale; on a lane, every pad is its hit.
  • Lights: the notes the FM-1 plays.
  • Steps, mixer, scale, play and stop, each edit one undo step on the FM-1.

While the Launchpad is plugged in, its pads go to the app, not to the tracks as a keyboard.
