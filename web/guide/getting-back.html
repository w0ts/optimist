• To the official firmware: in Install, step 4, take the copy of M-VAVE's FM-1 V15 the site keeps (or your own FM-1.fwsc from m-vave.com, unchanged) and install it. Or use M-VAVE's own updater, M-UPGRADE. The official firmware does not read Optimist's data: back it up first.
  • The FM-1 does not start Optimist: hold OCT− alone while switching on (OPTIMIST USB RESCUE), then install again. After two start-ups that crash, the FM-1 goes there by itself.
  • Switching between Optimist's own firmware and the release, or from any firmware: the installer picks the way itself (the running firmware's updater, else the chip's download mode through M-Flasher, a full backup first): docs/INSTALL-PATHS.md. No detour through V15.
  • Nothing starts at all: hold OCT− + OCT+ while switching on and keep them held for 3 s: the chip's own update mode (UBOOT; the screen stays dark). Then M-UPGRADE, or tools/fm1_rescue.py from Optimist's repository (macOS; from X0X: it backs up the whole flash first and writes only the firmware area), or FM-1-transporter. With the RP2040 dongle of tools/dongle/ (the transporter on a Seeed XIAO RP2040 or a Raspberry Pi Pico, three wires on the USB port) tools/fm1_rescue.py --dongle needs neither the key combo nor a cable swap: the dongle keys the chip when the FM-1 is switched on and is its USB host.
