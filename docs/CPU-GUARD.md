# The predictive CPU guard (`CPU_GUARD`)

Builder item `CPU_GUARD` (System; off by default, **on in the user-default and synth profiles**), `FELUCCA_CPU_GUARD`.
After Flowstate's guard by Zakaria Chowdhury (`zakariachowdhury/flowstate-fm1`, GPL-3.0-only): its CPU part, reworked.
Code: `firmware/src/system/cpuguard.c`, `cpuguard.h`; model `cpuguard_costs.h`, generated by `tools/builder/cpu_costs.py`;
tests `tests/cpuguard_test.c`, `tests/overload_stress.c`. Cost (`tools/builder/costs.json`): +3,372 B flash, +224 B RAM,
+144 B RAMTEXT (the code runs from XIP, called FAR once a half). Timing data come from the emulator (instructions, not
cycles) and host builds; **nothing is measured on an FM-1.**

## What it does

`audio.c` calls `cg_pre()` before each DMA half and `cg_post(us)` after it. The **load** is the larger of the half's
measured time and a prediction: the last half's measured load plus what the cost model says changed (voices started or
ended). It **steps up one level** when the load stays over 85 % (`CG_CEIL`) for 8 halves (`CG_HOLD`, 46 ms); or a half
runs late right after one over 85 %; or after a half over 85 % the next is predicted late (the step happens before it
renders). A lone late half after a normal one is a spike (a flash write, another interrupt) and steps nothing. A level with
nothing to ease in what sounds now is skipped (`cg_useful`); the half right after a step never steps on a prediction.

Levels, in order:
1. **Quality**: ACID without oversampling; ANALOG 2's swarm at 2 copies; drum tails end sooner (synth drum voice under
   -40 dBFS, not -72; X0X 808 voice 40 dB under its peak, not 60); the drum bus's DIST / FILTER count at 2/5 (L / R only).
2. **UNISON**: a UNISON part plays at most 2 voices; the extra voices fade out at the step.
3. **Reverb** (`fx.c rev_guard`): the bus goes off: wet fades over one half, the tank stops, its line is cleared 512
   samples a block from XIP; on release the send returns into a silent tank. (A running reverb costs about as much as the
   rest of an idle mix.) REV RET at 0 turns the bus off the same way in any build; CHO RET / DLY RET at 0 stop theirs.
4. **Notes**: a half over 85 % sheds one voice before the next; a half predicted late fades out as many voices as bring
   the prediction under 85 %, at most 4. Never shed: MONO, LEGATO or UNISON parts (bass and lead), a part with one voice,
   the drums, the only voice still held.

**Release**: a level comes off after 2 s (`CG_RELEASE` 345 halves) under 80 %, counting what the level saved (the drop in
the measured load over the 8 halves after its step), so it does not come and go every 2 s; the reverb level comes off only
if the reverb is already off.

**Reactive path** (`cg_spike`): late halves among lighter ones (the FM-1 adds them; its log at ~41-63 % mean load showed
12-17 a second) meet neither rule above. `audio.c` reports a half that ran late (the DMA moved on); each adds 64 to a
count that loses 1 a half; at 160 (about 3 within ~30 halves) with a mean load of 50 % or more the guard reacts: one
level up, or at the top one voice cut and the voices capped one under what sounded (`CG_VBUDGET`, voice.c; a new note
steals), the cap a voice higher after 2 s with no late half. A level a reaction set comes off only under the load the
drop-outs came at less 1/16 of a half, and not while voices are capped. In the host stress model of the log's signature the
late halves fall from 8.2 % to 1 %.

Under the ceiling the guard does nothing: the host never steps; `regress` built with it renders every golden unchanged.
**Visible**: SYSTEM > INFO (CPU lines with KNOB 1 NOW / MAX 5S / MAX, DROPS, UNDERRUNS, and in `SYS_DEBUG` builds GUARD: the
level, `MAX n` while capped, the voices shed; SHIFT + YES clears). The CDC console `status` prints `cpu_guard`,
`cpu_guard_steps`, `_shed`, `_early`, `_avg_pct`, `_step_pct`, `_rev_gain_pct` and `audio_late`.

## The cost model

Target instructions a sample, in `cpuguard_costs.h`: `CG_COST_BASE` 153 (idle mix), `CG_COST_DRUMS` 525 (the groove),
`CG_VCOST[uid]` per sounding voice (ANALOG 166, DIGITAL 134, PHASE 146, LOFI 93, SAMPLE 75, VOICE 241, TRIO 162, WHEEL 121,
GRAIN 115, FM6 196, SLICE / PHYS / CZ 751, ACID 490, JUNO 204, NOISE 136, DRONE 751), `CG_X0X[24]` per sounding X0X channel (909's 11
voices, 808's 13 lanes) and `CG_X0X_SHARED` 216 once while any sounds. Inserts count while their amount is set or they fade out:

| Term | Cost |
|---|---|
| a part's DIST / FILTER / DUST / DYN comp / CEIL limiter | 46 / 36 / 50 / 31 / 76 |
| the drum bus's DIST / FILTER / DUST / DYN comp | 128 / 184 / 73 / 69 |
| a drum voice's DIST / DYN; `CG_COST_DVINS[12]` FILT DUST FOLD FUZZ PHSR FRMT SFLD WRAP RECT HARM RING SLEW | 96 / 142; 74..182 |
| `CG_COST_PINS[18]` (a part) and `CG_COST_DBINS[18]` (drum bus): FOLD FUZZ PHSR FLNG FRMT WIDE CHRS ECHO GATER SFLD WRAP RECT HARM RING SUB SLEW TAPE SPIT | 25..97; TAPE 291 / 279, SPIT 268 / 213 |
| JUNO CHORUS, an instance (`CG_COST_JUNO`, not yet a `cpu_costs.py` entry) | 140 |

WRAP (id 20) is reserved and never built (entries 0); SLEW on a part is 0 (not measured). The master's two insert slots
(`cg_ins_mst`) cost what they do on the drum bus (DIST / FILT at 2/5); that and JUNO CHORUS are first models, not
calibrated in the emulator.

**Inputs**: `tests/cpu_baseline.txt`, **measured on the Mac** (`cc -O2`, instructions per 44.1 kHz sample counted by the
kernel with `proc_pid_rusage`, ~1 % run to run; the check allows +25 %), written by `tests/regress.c` with
`BUDGET_UPDATE=1`: every engine x preset with 8 notes held (VOICE 4, ACID 1, JUNO 6), the idle mix, the drum groove,
`cpu/x0x/*`, and `cpu/fx/*` for the inserts (including `part_tape`, `part_spit`, `dbus_tape`, `dbus_spit`). And the `cpu`
section of `tools/builder/costs.json`: target instructions per host instruction, fitted in the emulator at 96 MHz on bench
scenarios (2026-10-06 / 07): `scale_pct` 104, FM6 118, ACID 111, X0X 122, each the smallest measured/model ratio so the model
stays at or under the measured load. A voice is the median over the engine's presets of (preset - idle) / voices; engines
with no baseline entry (SLICE, PHYS, CZ) take the largest measured voice. `measure_costs.py` rewrites the header after each
run and keeps the `cpu` section; `cpu_costs.py --check` (in the test suite) fails when the header is not what the inputs
give. To refresh: a `BUDGET_UPDATE=1` regress run, then `python3 tools/builder/cpu_costs.py`.

## Measured effect (emulator, 96 MHz, 2026-10-06..15)

Against SLOOP 2.3's `SHED_FADE` alone, late halves fall wherever a voice can be shed (FM6 x3 + drums 69 -> 8) and ACID x3 +
drums goes 98 -> 1 with no note lost (the quality level drops the 303's oversampling). Ending synth drum tails at -40 dBFS
cuts `drums_mix` from 80 to 71 % mean load in the 909 + 808 mix scenario. Under the ceiling renders are bit-identical with
and without the guard. Hardware: not measured.

## Open limits

- On a DUAL=2 build parts 2 and 3 render on the second core, so shedding their voices does little for the first core.
- Where the drums and the protected voices alone exceed the half (the X0X 808 with a busy mix at 96 MHz) nothing is left
  to shed.
