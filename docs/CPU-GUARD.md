# The predictive CPU guard (`CPU_GUARD`)

Builder item `CPU_GUARD` (System; off by default), flag `FELUCCA_CPU_GUARD`. After Flowstate's guard by Zakaria
Chowdhury (`zakariachowdhury/flowstate-fm1` `e62e186`, `firmware/src/guard.c`, `docs/guardrails.md` §2.5,
GPL-3.0-only): only its CPU part, reworked for Optimist. Code: `firmware/src/cpuguard.c`, `cpuguard.h`; the model:
`firmware/src/cpuguard_costs.h`, generated by `tools/builder/cpu_costs.py`; tests: `tests/cpuguard_test.c`.

All numbers come from the emulator (it counts instructions, not cycles) and host builds. Nothing has run on an FM-1.

## What it does

Without the guard, `audio.c` sheds one voice before the next half after any half that took more than 85 % of its
time. A single heavy half sheds a note. The shed can be the bass: three ACID lines lost 80 notes in 5 s in
scenario 7 below.

With the guard, `audio.c` calls `cg_pre()` before each DMA half and `cg_post(us)` after it.

- **The load.** It is the larger of two values:
  - the half's measured time;
  - a prediction: the last half's measured load, plus what the cost model says changed since (voices started or
    ended).

  The prediction starts from a measurement, so what the guard eased shows in it at once. The model only adds the
  change.
- **Step up.** The guard goes up one level when:
  - the load stays over 85 % for 8 halves in a row (46 ms); or
  - one half runs late (100 %); or
  - after a half over 85 %, the next half is predicted to run late. The step then happens before that half
    renders.

  A level with nothing to ease in what sounds now is skipped. An FM6-only mix goes straight to shedding.
- **The levels**, in order:
  1. **Quality**:
     - ACID renders without oversampling (X0X's lite mode of the 303, `bass303_set_lite`);
     - ANALOG 2's swarm plays at most 2 copies.
  2. **UNISON**: a UNISON part plays at most 2 voices. The extra voices fade out at the step, and new notes start
     2.
  3. **Notes**: as before, a half over 85 % sheds one voice before the next. A half predicted late fades out at
     once (2 blocks) as many voices as bring the prediction under 85 %, at most 4. Shedding never touches:
     - a MONO, LEGATO or UNISON part (the bass and the lead);
     - a monophonic engine (ACID);
     - the drums;
     - the only voice still held.
- **Release.** A level comes off after 2 s (345 halves) under 80 % (Flowstate's 1/16 under the ceiling). The guard
  adds what that level saved to the load before it compares: the drop in the measured load over the 8 halves after
  the step. So a level comes off only when the music it held back would fit again, and it does not come and go
  every 2 s. Levels with nothing left to ease come off with it.
- **On screen.** GLO > SYSTEM CPU shows `%G1`, `%G2` or `%G3` while a level holds. The CDC console prints
  `cpu_guard`, `cpu_guard_steps` and `cpu_guard_shed`.
- **Under the ceiling the guard does nothing.**
  - The host never steps: it has no clock.
  - `regress` built with the guard renders every golden unchanged.
  - In the emulator at 192 MHz, scenarios 2, 7 and 8 render the same audio bit for bit with and without the guard
    (8: at a 2-frame offset from boot timing). Scenario 1 differs only after 3.2 s: without the guard, one half at
    87 % shed a voice; the guard kept it. Neither had a late half.

## The cost model

`cpuguard_costs.h` holds target instructions a sample:
- the idle mix;
- the drum groove;
- one sounding voice per engine UID.

`tools/builder/cpu_costs.py` generates it; nothing in it is hand-tuned.
- **Inputs:**
  - `tests/cpu_baseline.txt`, which `regress` writes (`BUDGET_UPDATE=1`);
  - the `cpu` section of `tools/builder/costs.json`.
- **A voice:** the median over the engine's presets of (preset − idle mix) / its voices (8; VOICE 4, ACID 1).
- **Engines with no baseline entry** (SLICE, PHYS: not built when the baseline was taken) take the largest measured
  voice.
- **Re-runs:** `measure_costs.py` writes the header again after each run and keeps the `cpu` section.
- **Check:** `cpu_costs.py --check` (in `make test`) fails when the header is not what its inputs give.

**Calibration** (`costs.json` `cpu`, 2026-10-06). The host counts Mac instructions and the target counts pi32v2
instructions. In the emulator at 96 MHz, the guard ran on scenarios 1, 2, 3, 7 and 8 with its actions switched off.
It recorded its model's load against the measured load:

| Scenario | Measured / model (at 100 %) |
|---|---|
| 1: FM6 ×3 (8 voices) + drums | 1.236 |
| 2: ANALOG SUPER PAD ×3 | 1.042 |
| 3: FM6, ANALOG, FM6 | 1.153 |
| 7: ACID ×3 + drums | 1.117 |
| 8: FM6 MARK I 8-note cluster + drums | 1.181 |

Each scale is the smallest of these ratios, so the model stays at or under the measured load:
- `scale_pct` 104 (all scenarios);
- FM6 118 (scenarios 1 and 8);
- ACID 111 (scenario 7).

## Cost

Measured builds (`measure_costs.py --only CPU_GUARD`, default configuration):
- **Flash:** +1,220 B. With ACID built: +2,484 B, because the 303's lite path is compiled in too.
- **RAM:** +64 B.
- **RAMTEXT:** +180 B. The guard's code runs from XIP: it is called FAR once a half. The 180 B come from a few
  bytes in the render (the swarm and UNISON caps) and from the layout of the globals moving with the guard's
  state.
- **CPU:** 0.05 % at 96 MHz. `FM1_HOT` on scenario 8 counted 97,659 instructions in `cg_est`, `cg_pre` and
  `cg_post` over 2 s, of 192 M.

`CPU_GUARD` is off in every profile:
- user-default has 44 B of flash free;
- everything-that-fits has 76 B of RAMTEXT free.

## Measurements

Emulator `feat/upstream-merge` (`play_check`, `FM1_CPU_MHZ=96`). Each run is 1 s of boot, then 4 s recorded.
- **Builds:** user-default without the PIANO set (room for the bench code); scenario 7 adds ACID.
- **Scenarios** are in `bench.c`. They are measurement builds only, never shipped. Scenario 8 is new.
- **Counters:**
  - late halves: `felucca_dbg.late`, the DMA moved on before the render ended;
  - halves: the halves rendered;
  - sheds: `shed_count`, or the guard's own count.

| Scenario at 96 MHz | Before: late / sheds | Guard: late / sheds | Guard's highest level |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1: FM6 ×3, 8 voices + drums | 25 / 42 | 8 / 25 | notes |
| 2: ANALOG SUPER PAD ×3 + drums | 1 / 23 | 1 / 14 | notes (after quality) |
| 3: FM6, ANALOG, FM6 + drums | 20 / 39 | 6 / 27 | notes (after quality) |
| 7: ACID ×3 lines + drums | 31 / 80 | 1 / 0 | notes (nothing to shed: the bass lines stay) |
| 8: FM6 MARK I 8-note cluster + drums | 21 / 35 | 5 / 24 | notes |

- **Why fewer late halves:** the notes level fades voices out before a half that the prediction says will run
  late. Shedding alone releases one voice a half, and a long release keeps costing.
- **Why ACID gets back in time:** the quality level turns off its oversampling. No ACID line loses a note.
- **Not measured:**
  - X0X's 808 groove: `feat/x0x-drums` is not merged yet.
  - The guard on hardware (XIP cache and flash wait states).
